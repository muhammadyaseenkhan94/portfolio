About me

Turning business processes into reliable Oracle applications

I develop Oracle and PL/SQL based applications across the full Oracle stack: database objects and complex PL/SQL, Oracle Forms & Reports, and modern Oracle APEX applications with REST integrations.

I have delivered systems for healthcare, textile and garments, ERP, finance and document workflows, for companies in Pakistan and for international clients.

Every application I build starts with the business process, not the screen. I sit with the people who do the work, whether that is a store keeper issuing fabric, an HR officer approving leave or an accountant closing the month, and map how data really moves: who enters it, who checks it, who approves it and which report the management needs at the end. That picture becomes the data model, and the data model becomes the application.

On the database side I design normalised tables with proper keys, constraints and indexes, and keep the business rules close to the data in PL/SQL packages, so the same logic serves APEX pages, Oracle Forms, scheduled jobs and REST APIs alike. On the front end I use Oracle APEX to turn that logic into clean, role-based screens: interactive grids for fast data entry, validations that stop mistakes before they are saved, approval workflows, dashboards and printable reports.

Reliability matters more than features. I test with real volumes of data, tune slow queries with Bulk Collect and proper indexing, handle exceptions so users see clear messages instead of error codes, and secure every page with authentication, authorization schemes and session state protection. When a legacy Oracle Forms system has reached its limits, I migrate it to APEX step by step so that daily operations keep running during the move.

Talented, ambitious and self-motivated, I bring a strong technical background, sound self-discipline and the ability to deliver quality work with minimal supervision. A quick learner who readily absorbs new ideas and communicates clearly and effectively, I am seeking to join a dynamic and growing organisation to build a career as a first-class software developer.

How I turn a process into an application

  1. 01
    Understand the process

    Meet the users, study current paper forms and spreadsheets, and agree on the exact flow, roles and approvals.

  2. 02
    Design the data model

    Tables, relationships, constraints and codes that match the business, built to stay consistent as data grows.

  3. 03
    Write the business logic

    PL/SQL packages, procedures and triggers for calculations, postings and validations, reusable across every screen.

  4. 04
    Build the APEX application

    Forms, interactive grids, dashboards, reports and workflows with role-based access and a consistent Universal Theme UI.

  5. 05
    Integrate and test

    REST APIs through ORDS, third-party services such as ZATCA, and testing with real data before go-live.

  6. 06
    Deploy, train and support

    Smooth deployment, user training and ongoing enhancements as the business changes.
